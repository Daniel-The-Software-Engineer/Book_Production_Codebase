# The Lost Archive

## Chapter 1 – The Letter

On the morning of her twenty-first birthday, Maya received a sealed envelope with no return address. Inside was a single handwritten sentence:

> "The truth was never lost. It was hidden."

The message referred to an old family archive that everyone believed had burned down years ago. Although her grandparents refused to discuss it, Maya became determined to discover what had really happened.

She packed a notebook, a flashlight, and a camera before travelling to the abandoned estate where the archive had once stood.

---

## Chapter 2 – The Locked Library

The estate appeared empty, but one room remained untouched by time.

Rows of dusty books lined the walls, and a heavy wooden door stood at the back of the library. An unusual symbol had been carved into the lock—a design Maya had seen inside the mysterious letter.

After several failed attempts, she discovered a hidden mechanism behind one of the shelves. The door slowly opened, revealing a narrow staircase leading underground.

--- 

## Chapter 3 – The Hidden Chamber

At the bottom of the staircase, Maya discovered a small stone chamber illuminated by narrow beams of sunlight filtering through cracks in the ceiling.

Dust covered every surface, yet the room looked strangely untouched. Shelves filled with carefully preserved journals surrounded a large wooden table, and a single leather-bound book rested in the center as if someone had left it there only moments before.

As Maya reached for the book, she noticed fresh footprints leading toward a doorway on the opposite side of the chamber. She realized she was not the first person to discvoer the archive.
