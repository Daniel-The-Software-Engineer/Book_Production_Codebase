# Character Profile: Maya

## Basic Information

**Name:** Maya  
**Age:** 21  
**Role:** Protagonist

## Background

Maya has always been curious about her family's past. After receiving a mysterious letter on her twenty-first birthday, she begins investigating the long-lost family archive that everyone believed had been destroyed years ago.

## Personality

- Curious
- Determined
- Resourceful
- Observant
- Courageous

## Skills

- Excellent problem-solving abilities
- Pays close attention to detail
- Keeps thorough investigation notes
- Enjoys researching historical records

## Motivation

Maya wants to uncover the truth about the missing archive and understand why her family has kept its history a secret.

## Current Status

Maya has discovered a hidden chamber beneath the abandoned estate and realizes that someone else has recently been inside the archive.
